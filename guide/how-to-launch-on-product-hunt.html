How to Launch on Product Hunt in 2026 (Without a Big Following)

Product Hunt is still the single highest-leverage launch you can do — a top-5 finish drives signups, backlinks, and often press. You don't need a big following, but you do need to prepare.

Before launch day

Set up your product page in advance (you can save a draft). You need: a crisp tagline, a gallery (first image is everything), a clear first comment from the maker, and a short demo. Line up a hunter only if they're genuinely relevant — self-hunting is fine in 2026.

Timing

Launches run on a 24-hour cycle starting 12:01am PT. Tuesday–Thursday are competitive but high-traffic; a weekend launch faces less competition but lower overall traffic. Pick based on whether you want ranking or reach.

Launch day

Post early, reply to every comment fast, and share the link where your audience already is — not with 'please upvote' (against the rules) but with 'we're live, would love feedback'. The first few hours set your trajectory.
