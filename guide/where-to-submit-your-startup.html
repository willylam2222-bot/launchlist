How to actually do it

1) Prepare your assets once: a one-liner, a 3-sentence description, a longer description, a logo, and 2-3 screenshots. 2) Lead with value, not a bare link — many communities remove pure self-promo. 3) Prioritise dofollow links for SEO. 4) Space launches out; don't blast everything in an hour.
